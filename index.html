is a series of games based around a common social deduction mechanic.

Status
is a card that faces away from you, and others can see.
House
is a secret allegiance card to one of four houses.
Nobles
are placed in houses by completing various paths.

Call for the Throne — the game comes to a conclusion when a certain number of Nobles have gathered in a house. A player may then Call for the Throne. All players reveal their House and Status cards. The player with the highest Status in the House that called wins!
